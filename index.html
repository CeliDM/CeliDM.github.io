Research

My research examines the end-of-life phase of offshore wind farms, from decommissioning at sea to material recovery on land. The first generation of wind farms in the Belgian North Sea is approaching the end of its operational life, with few large-scale decommissioning projects as precedents. Decommissioning nonetheless requires cooperation between wind farm owners, marine contractors, recyclers and public authorities. Their decisions cover which structures are removed or left in situ, and how the recovered materials are treated. These choices affect how much material returns to the economy, the environmental impact of decommissioning and the supply of critical raw materials.

My dissertation quantifies the circularity, environmental impact and material criticality of alternative decommissioning and end-of-life strategies. It combines material flow analysis, circular economy metrics, life cycle assessment, cascade allocation modelling and material criticality assessment in scenarios for one of these first-generation wind farms.

Joint work with colleagues extends the analysis to the diverging economic incentives of wind farm owners and marine contractors, and to the European stocks and flows of metals in wind turbines. The ORESA, DECOMPASS and CTO projects bring the work into direct contact with wind farm operators, recyclers, engineering firms and public agencies. Master's thesis projects linked to this work cover subsea cables, second-life options for wind turbines and raw material demand for electrification infrastructure.

Projects

  • ORESA

    Offshore Renewable Energy Sustainability Alliance. Interreg North-West Europe, 2025-2028

    A consortium of industry, government and research partners from five North-West European countries that works on sustainability in offshore renewable energy, including circularity and life cycle assessment.

  • DECOMPASS

    Decommissioning Compass for Offshore Wind. Energy Transition Fund, 2025-2028

    A quantitative model to compare decommissioning and processing scenarios for Belgian offshore wind turbines on economic and environmental sustainability. Coordinated by POM West-Vlaanderen.

  • CTO

    Circular Transition in Offshore Wind. VLAIO, 2022-2024

    A full life cycle analysis of offshore wind turbines that prepares the sector for the retirement of wind farms. The project covered decommissioning scenarios, material choices, material flows and a Building Information Model for offshore wind turbines.
